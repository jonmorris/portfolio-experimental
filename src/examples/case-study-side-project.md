## What it is

Open with the hook — one or two sentences on what this is and why it's worth a look. The role/year/link above do the framing; say it's self-initiated and move on.

Tally is a habit tracker with exactly one screen: a grid of dots you tap to fill in. No streaks, no notifications, no account. I designed and built it for myself over a couple of weekends.

## Why I built it

Lead with **motivation**, not a business problem — passion projects don't have a client or a P&L. What itch were you scratching? What did existing tools get wrong for you?

Every habit app I tried turned a simple daily checkmark into a guilt machine of broken-streak red. I wanted the opposite: a calm record of what I did, with nothing nagging me about what I didn't.

State is just `localStorage`, so there's no sign-up and nothing leaves the device. The hardest design call was resisting every feature I wanted to add.

## What I learned

Close with one honest reflection or what's next — the side-project version of an outcomes section. Qualitative signal ("a few friends use it daily") beats inventing metrics you don't have.

Constraint is a feature: shipping *one screen* made me actually use it where heavier apps got deleted. A few friends are on it now; if I take it further, the next experiment is optional weekly notes without bringing back the streak guilt.
