## What it is

NHL-95 Rosters is a side project focused on organizing and presenting roster data in a way that's easier to browse and reference.

## Why I built it

I wanted a cleaner and more useful way to work with NHL-95 roster information than scattered notes and ad-hoc files.

## How it works

The project structures roster data so it can be explored quickly, with a clear layout and straightforward navigation.

## What I learned

Working on this reinforced how much information architecture and clarity matter, even for niche data-heavy hobby projects.
