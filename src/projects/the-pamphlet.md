## Why I built it

I was in the middle of baking something, I needed a liquid measurement converted. I googled "liquid measurement conversions" and found only despair. There are many many results. I clicked a website, no conversion chart within a scroll, it's an ad farm cooking website with an entire article before the conversion chart. I go back and look at images, find one that looks promising — it's inaccurate. I finished what I was cooking, sat down and started making my own chart.

Pamphlet was created to fix the SEO optimized nonsense cluttering up someone's search for basic information.

## What it is

I designed and developed this site to house common information that is somehow difficult to obtain on the modern internet. It's a pretty simple setup for now with only a few content pages. I'm building it organically as I find something else I want to recreate in this form.

## Design

The ethos of this site is "simple, fast, and accurate". So, I wanted the brand to reflect that fact. It gets out of the way, it's clean and sharp, but subdued.
