## What it is

Board game rules have always lived on paper. [Cardboard Appendix](https://www.cardboardappendix.com) is what they look like as a real digital product: searchable, cross-linked, accessible, and responsive, with as many copies at the table as you need. I designed and built it solo, with permissions in place from publishers and game designers, and a public relaunch coming in 2026.

## Why I built it

I created Cardboard Appendix to address the shortcomings of existing board game rulebooks. The idea is to take static paper board game rulebooks and move them into a more flexible, responsive, accessible, searchable, cross references, etc. form.

## How it works

This is currently in a very developmental discovery and exploration phase. I want to try and find the best way(s) to present this kind of information in a highly usable way.
