## Overview

At Fidelity, I worked on the design of an enterprise architecture capability tree. An internal tool that helps teams navigate, understand, and assess the organization's complex technology ecosystem. The product was intended to track tech usage, company dispositions on if a given tool was approved for use, and to serve as a quick way to help a user find the data they needed related to those items.

I provided ongoing design support for three agile development teams while also leading broader usability initiatives. When I joined, the product was midway through its lifecycle and lacked rigorous UX foundations, so I focused on strengthening its information architecture to make complex capabilities easier to navigate and understand. I introduced usability and accessibility improvements, created and maintained a dedicated design system, and ran targeted research projects to better align the product with user needs. This work not only supported day-to-day delivery but also enabled faster stakeholder decision-making and set the product up to scale effectively for the long term.
