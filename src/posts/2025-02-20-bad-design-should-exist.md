---
title: "Bad Design Should Exist"
date: 2025-02-20
tags: ["post"]
---

I believe there are certain things that shouldn't be good. They should not be well designed. Or maybe this is just saying that the same level of polish, of thought, shouldn't be applied to everything. Or maybe it's a problem of branding, certain things should be grungy - that's just part of their brand.

Think dive bars, if you go into a place that looks like a dive bar. But you get inside and it's a little too clean, things aren't sticky for no reason, if the bathroom is tidy and nothing is broken - this isn't a dive bar. It's a place trying to take on the aura of a dive bar, but it's failing. It's not bad enough.

The first time I thought about this was in the case of a homebrew supply store, that sells gear and ingredients for brewing beer at home. There's just something about a good grungy homebrew shop that is lost if the place feels too clean, organized, and corporate. It's just the wrong vibe, it's offputting.
