A few years ago I think it was genuinely impressive that, as a designer, I could also do substantial front-end development — the rare designer who could actually build the thing, not just hand off a file. But the list kept growing. Somewhere along the way I became someone who can sell the work, define the project, gather the requirements, run product, design end to end from UX to visual, build the front end, manage the project, shape the process, lead and mentor a team — and now, with AI, vibecode and ship entire products of my own. Here's what I keep getting stuck on: is all of that impressive, or does it just look like I'm spread too thin? None of it is *un*impressive on its own — obviously not. But does the breadth read as range, or as a red flag: a generalist who can't possibly be deep in any one of it? The question I'm actually circling is harder than "what can I do." It's: how do I *prove* I can do all of it — and convince someone that depth and breadth aren't a trade?
