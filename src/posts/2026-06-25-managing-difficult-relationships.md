This post is about dealing with interpersonal difficulty at work — the part of the job that has nothing to do with the work itself. I want to dig into how you handle friction with the different kinds of people you're in it with: coworkers and peers, clients, and the trickiest of all, people who are senior to you. The dynamics aren't the same across those relationships — what you can say, how directly you can push, and what's at stake all shift depending on who's on the other side. The thread I want to pull on is how to navigate that well: staying honest and effective without burning the relationship, and figuring out what changes (and what doesn't) when the difficulty is with a superior versus a client versus a coworker.
