# DESIGN_INSPIRATION.md is the design canon

_Decided 2026-06-24._

Design, layout, typography, color and motion decisions are checked against `DESIGN_INSPIRATION.md` first, and it outranks generic best practice and outside research unless the owner overrides it. This keeps the look coherent as the site grows and stops well-meaning "best practice" from flattening it. See `CLAUDE.md` → Design canon.
