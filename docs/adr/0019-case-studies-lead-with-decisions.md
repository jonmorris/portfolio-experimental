# Case studies lead with decisions, not process

_Decided 2026-11-26._

Work case studies follow a decisions-first structure: an opening that states the problem, what shipped and what changed; a role-and-scope block; an optional confidentiality note; the problem; just enough process to explain the constraints; the design decisions as the main body, each with the options rejected and what was lost; the outcome, including what didn't move; and what I'd do differently. It replaces the earlier problem → research → process → solution → outcome spine. The target is senior and lead product designer roles, and the newer first-party hiring guidance (HubSpot 2025, Atlassian 2023, Meta 2019) says process is over-shown and asks for decisions and trade-offs, while the process-first advice the old spine followed comes from NN/g (2019), which Atlassian argues against directly. Sources: `docs/research/senior-product-designer-case-studies-verified.md`. ADR-0009 (one template for work and side projects) still stands.

## Consequences

Existing case studies written to the old spine (Better Trail, E.L.F.) no longer match the template; they're brought in line when they're chosen and revised (#23), not retrofitted now. Unsourced figures the old template carried (a ~60-second read, 800–1,500 words, ~30% imagery) were dropped.
