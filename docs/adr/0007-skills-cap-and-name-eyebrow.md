# Five skills per project, and the name as an eyebrow

_Decided 2026-06-24._

A project lists at most five `skills`. A short, curated list scans in the ~60 seconds a reader gives a card and forces a real choice about what each project demonstrates; long tech lists read as noise. On detail pages, the outcome-focused `title` is the headline and the project/client `name` moves to a small eyebrow above it: the headline sells, the eyebrow identifies.
